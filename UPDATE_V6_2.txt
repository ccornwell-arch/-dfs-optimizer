DFS Lab V6.2 — Contest Intelligence

Changes
- Scenario influence is now a true 0–100 slider.
- Added Entry Format: Single Entry, 3-Max, 20-Max, 150-Max.
- Entry format and field size now change Showdown tournament aggression.
- Added contest-aware soft role-confidence penalty: fragile cheap punts are penalized more in Single Entry and less in large-field formats; they are never automatically banned.
- Ownership leverage is not used in the optimizer objective when ownership is missing.
- Added Lineup Lab to select a generated lineup and explain it in the context of THIS slate, game thesis, field size, entry format, construction and Captain.
- Added first What-If contrast: replace one lineup player with another and immediately see projection/salary impact and whether a second move is required.
- Historical explanation is moved out of the primary WHY workflow; history remains under the projection engine.
- Improved dark-background text contrast.
- Removed stale V5 language from Scenario help and updated visible version to V6.2.
