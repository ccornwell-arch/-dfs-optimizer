01 / HOW IT WORKS

From salary file to tournament-ready lineups.

Three steps. Your assumptions stay in control from the first upload to the final export.

STEP 01

Upload your DraftKings salaries CSV.

Start with the official contest file. Aytia reads the player pool, positions, teams, and salaries.

STEP 02

Set your game story and player rules.

Choose who wins, whether the game shoots out or grinds, then lock, fade, or exclude players as needed.

STEP 03

Generate, review, and export.

Compare lineup grades and game-world stories, then download a DraftKings-format CSV when the build feels right.

02 / BUILT FOR GPPs

An optimizer that understands your thesis.

Aytia turns football logic into lineup construction, then shows you how each build holds up.

Game-script engine
